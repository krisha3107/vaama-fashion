About Vaama Fashion

A clothing store in Adajan, Surat.

Who we are

Vaama Fashion is a clothing store located in Adajan, Surat, Gujarat. The store offers a wide variety of fashionable clothing for everyday wear and special occasions, organised into easy-to-browse categories.

From casual T-shirts and Korean-style pants to festive Ghaghra Choli and seasonal collections, Vaama Fashion brings a curated range of styles to customers in Surat and the surrounding area.

What we offer

  • One-Piece dresses and outfits
  • Two-Piece co-ord sets
  • Denims — jeans and denim styles
  • Korean Pants — straight-cut, wide-leg and cargo styles
  • Shirts and T-Shirts for everyday wear
  • Frocks for all occasions
  • Seasonal Collections including Ghaghra Choli
