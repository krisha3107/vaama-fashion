How to add a real product photo

  1. Place the photo file in assets/images/products/, e.g. VF-001.jpg.
  2. Go to Products, click Edit on the product.
  3. In the Image path field type: assets/images/products/VF-001.jpg
  4. Click Save changes.
  5. Open the product page in the shop to confirm the photo appears.
